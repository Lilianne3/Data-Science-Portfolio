## About Me

I'm Lillie Fitzsimmons, a biology student at Oregon State University interested in microbiology, ecology, and marine science. I earned my Associate of Science at Front Range Community College, and my long-term goal is to pursue a PhD in marine microbiology. I want to study microbial communities and how they interact with their environments.

My research experience includes designing an antimicrobial study comparing live Lactobacillus plantarum cultures and cell-free supernatants against foodborne pathogens. In a molecular virology laboratory, I also analyzed sequencing results from seven plasmids to verify constructs and assess mutations. Those experiences helped me see how much careful interpretation matters when working with biological data.

I'm interested in data science because biological research often involves large datasets, including DNA sequencing and environmental sampling. I've learned some Python, and I want more practice using it with actual data. I'm especially interested in data cleaning, visualization, and choosing appropriate statistical methods. My experience with survey research, science writing, and disability advocacy also shapes how I think about communicating findings clearly and making science more accessible.
