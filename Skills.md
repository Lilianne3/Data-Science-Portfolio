Job Skills Notes

Posting 1: Computational Biologist 1
Level: Level 1, but still requires a master's or a bachelor's with three years of relevant experience.
Required:
- Computational biology degree or a related field, with the experience listed above.
- Omics analysis for finding treatment targets or biomarkers.
- Statistics, including data quality checks, normalization, and identifying differences in data.
- Building reproducible, scalable pipelines and managing data.
- Strong programming skills, ideally R or Python.
- Designing and building systems and pipelines using high performance computing.
- Prioritizing multiple tasks.
Preferred:
- Relevant coursework and single-cell analysis.
- PhD in computational biology, bioinformatics, or a related field.
- Proteomics knowledge, including mass spectrometry data.
- Machine learning experience.
- Interest in open-access innovation.
Also mentioned in the duties:
- Organization, people skills, independent project management, helping lead projects, preparing and presenting data, and working with an interdisciplinary team.

Posting 2: Remote Sensing Data Scientist
Level: Experienced role working alongside senior scientists.
Required:
- PhD, master's with three or more years of industry experience, or bachelor's with five or more years.
- Building geospatial data products and using data from new imaging instruments.
- Solving problems from basic scientific principles.
- Strong Python skills, including tools like COGs, Xarray, and GDAL.
- Written and verbal communication.
- Working with interdisciplinary teams in different locations.
- Prioritizing and adapting in a fast-paced startup.
Preferred:
- Processing multispectral or infrared satellite images.
- Knowledge of wildfire monitoring, weather, or environmental remote sensing.
- Satellite product development, commissioning, early operations, or delivering working pipelines to customers.
- Machine learning, deep learning, and advanced image processing.
- Cloud-based geospatial workflows and large-scale processing, preferably AWS.
- Writing technical designs, product specifications, or explanations of algorithms.
Also mentioned in the duties:
- Building scalable algorithms, turning prototypes into production code, connecting code to data services, product planning, checking scientific accuracy, documentation, customer communication, collaborating with engineers, and learning new topics.

Skills in Both:
- Programming and scientific data analysis.
- Scalable pipelines.
- Teamwork and communication.
- Prioritizing tasks.
- Machine learning (preferred in both).

What Stood Out:
- I expected less overlap between cancer research and satellite imaging, but both jobs wanted coding, data analysis, and teamwork. I was surprised that machine learning was preferred rather than required in both. The Level 1 position also asked for more education or experience than I expected from the title.
